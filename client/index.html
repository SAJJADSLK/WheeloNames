Free Spin Wheel Maker & Random Name Picker

Wheeloname is a free, interactive spin wheel generator designed for classrooms, raffles, giveaways, party games, and team decisions. Add unlimited names, spin the wheel, and pick fair winners with satisfying sound effects and celebration animations.

Key Features

  • Customizable Spin Wheels: Add unlimited entries, customize theme colors, and save wheels in your browser.
  • Classroom Student Picker: Select students randomly and fairly with optional elimination after each spin.
  • Raffle & Prize Draws: Conduct provably fair prize giveaways for live streams, social media, and events.
  • Weighted Probability: Assign custom weights to entries for uneven probability scenarios.
  • Instant Team Division: Split entries into balanced groups, teams, or pairs with one click.
  • Import & Export: Easily import names from spreadsheets and export winner logs to CSV.

How to Use Wheeloname

  1. Add Your Entries: Type or paste your list of names, numbers, or choices into the wheel editor.
  2. Customize Your Settings: Select a color theme, toggle sound effects, and choose multi-draw or eliminate-on-win options.
  3. Spin & Celebrate: Click the wheel or spin button to randomly select a winner with celebration effects.
